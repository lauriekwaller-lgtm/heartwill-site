Heartwill

Your emotional will, built as life happens.

Not everyone leaves a big legacy. Everyone can leave a personal one.

Heartwill is a simple place to save your words, wisdom, photos and small moments for the people you love. You add to it as life goes on, a little at a time. Whatever happens, they’ll have what you wanted them to know.

No sitting down to write a goodbye letter. Just the good things, kept safe and organized for each person in your life.

When a few friends passed suddenly, it made me think about my own family and the words I want them to have someday. I always meant to write it all down, but a goodbye letter felt too heavy. So I made Heartwill, a way to save those words a little at a time, as life happens.

Laurie, founder
