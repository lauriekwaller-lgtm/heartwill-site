Thank you for helping me test Heartwill

You’re one of the first people to try it, before anyone else sees it.

Heartwill is a simple place to save notes, photos, favorite sayings and saved texts for the people you love, a little at a time, as life happens. Each person gets their own envelope.

Keep things in their envelope for now, send them today just because, or have them arrive on a birthday or anniversary. They open it from an email link, with no app or account needed, and only the people you choose ever see what you’ve saved.

Your memories are private. Heartwill doesn’t show me anything you save. In Heartwill, only you, and anyone you choose to send something to, can see it.

Not just for parents

Heartwill is for anyone you’d want to say something to.

  • Your partner: an anniversary envelope you add to all year.
  • A best friend: the inside jokes, photos and funny texts you’d hate to lose.
  • A niece or nephew: stories and advice for graduation day.
  • A friend getting married: a year of memories, given on the wedding day.
  • A sibling: the childhood moments only the two of you remember.
  • Someone you mentor: the advice you wish someone had given you.
  • Just because: send a full envelope on an ordinary Tuesday.

What I’d love you to do (about 10 minutes)

  1. Tap Try Heartwill below and sign in with your email. You’ll get an email with a button to tap. No password.
  2. Add one person you love, and save one real thing for them: a note, a photo, or something they always say.
  3. Choose Send it now and use your own email, so you can see exactly what they would get.
